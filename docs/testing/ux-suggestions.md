# UX Suggestions: User-Friendliness Only (No New Features)

**Rules applied:**
- Simple Bangla that ordinary shop owners and staff understand, not machine-translated text.
- Numbers always in English digits.
- No mixed Bangla/English labels.
- Inputs block wrong characters.

## B. Input validation (block wrong input)

| Screen | Field | Problem | Suggestion |

## D. Verified OK (passed)

- Cash In amount input blocks letters and keeps 2 decimals; future dates are blocked.
- Cash Out above the balance is blocked; double-tap creates only 1 withdrawal; the partner is fixed to the logged-in user.
- Partner form blocks a spaces-only name.
- Discount: % filters letters, >90% blocked, end date can't be before start.
- PO lot quantity above ordered shows a Bangla error.
- PO landed cost: (18,000 + 500 + 50) / 9 good = ৳2,061.11 ✓; the damaged unit's cost is absorbed by the good units.
- Weighted average: (5×2000 + 9×2061.11)/14 ≈ ৳2,039.28 ✓ (rounding, see B-31).
- POS: the discount is applied automatically (৳2,500 → ৳2,250); quantity is capped at stock; Part Paid ৳1,000 cash + ৳1,000 bKash leaves ৳2,500 due ✓; double-click confirm creates 1 sale ✓; stock 13 → 11 ✓; profit ৳421.44 ✓.
- Due: over-collection blocked; ৳500 collection leaves ৳2,000 due ✓.
- Cash Book: 185,000 + 102,500 − 23,800 = 263,700 ✓; cash/bKash split ✓; the payment made from the partner's own pocket is correctly not in the cash book.
- Dashboard: sales ৳4,500, profit ৳421.44, margin 9.4% ✓.
